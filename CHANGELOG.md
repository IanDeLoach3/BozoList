# Changelog

## 1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Medieval parchment ledger UI with custom dark-leather/gold buttons.
- Add/remove players with a reason and date; account-wide saved list.
- Automatic warnings on whisper (with inline chat tag), target, mouseover, and group/raid join.
- On-screen warning banner + sound (15s per-person cooldown).
- Bottom-right "nearby" pop-up driven by a vanilla-compatible nameplate scanner.
- Full support for Forever's two-name ("First Last") system, including forgiving first-name matching.
